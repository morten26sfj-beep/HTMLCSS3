OUR COSMIC NEIGHBOURS

The Solar System

The solar system contains one star, eight planets and several dwarf planets, including Pluto.

The Sun

Our star and the centre of the solar system.

Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun.

Venus

The hottest planet because of its thick atmosphere.

Earth

Our home planet and the only known planet with life.

Mars

The red planet with a cold, rocky surface.

Jupiter

The largest planet in the solar system.

Saturn

A gas giant famous for its beautiful rings.

Uranus

An ice giant that rotates on its side.

Neptune

The furthest major planet from the Sun.

Pluto

A famous dwarf planet in the outer solar system.
