OUR COSMIC NEIGHBOURS

Planet Facts

Learn more about every major object in our solar system.

The Sun

The Sun is a star at the centre of our solar system. Its gravity keeps the planets in orbit.

Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest planet to the Sun. It has a very thin atmosphere.

Venus

Venus is the hottest planet in the solar system because of its thick atmosphere and strong greenhouse effect.

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet with life.

Mars

Mars is known as the red planet because iron minerals in its soil have created a reddish surface.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system. It is a gas giant with a famous storm called the Great Red Spot.

Saturn

Saturn is a gas giant surrounded by bright rings made from ice, dust and pieces of rock.

Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant that rotates at a very unusual angle, appearing to spin on its side.

Neptune

Neptune is the furthest major planet from the Sun and is known for its powerful winds.

Pluto

Pluto is a dwarf planet located in the Kuiper Belt beyond Neptune. It was reclassified in 2006.
